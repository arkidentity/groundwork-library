# Church services kickoff template

The start for a church we serve with automations, newsletters, sermon content and follow-up.
**Kept small on purpose.** Pastors who are already stretched feel a long list of questions as one
more pile. So the church sees three questions, one call, and a one-page plan.

Run it from the church's repo: say **"start a church-services project."**

## What the church sees

1. **Three questions before the call:** who's on this with you, what drains you, send us your sermon
   links and recent newsletters.
2. **One 60-minute call** (`kickoff-call-guide.md`): every task goes into Automate, Delegate, Own or
   Drop, and we pick the first one or two builds.
3. **"Your plan":** one page to review and approve.

That's the whole onboarding. After it, a question goes out only when a build needs the answer, one
at a time (`just-in-time.yml`). The AI policy is drafted from `ai-policy-outline.md` before the
first automation that writes to people.

## Never in a written item

Passwords or logins, members' names or contact details, prayer requests, giving amounts, and
anything about a staff conflict or a specific person.
