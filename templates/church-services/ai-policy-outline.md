# AI policy outline (team only)

Starting point for each church's AI policy. Write it in the church's voice from their q-ai answers,
keep it to one page, and share it as a review item. Their leaders adopt it before any automation
touches people.

1. **Why we use it.** To give our staff time back for people. Never to replace a person's care.
2. **What we use it for.** Drafting newsletters and announcements, sermon notes and clips, organizing
   follow-up, summarizing meetings. (Fill from the church's packages.)
3. **What we never use it for.** Prayer or pastoral replies sent as if from a person. Counseling.
   Decisions about people. (Add what the church named in q-ai.)
4. **A person approves.** Every message to a person is reviewed by a named role before it goes out.
   List any message the leaders have agreed may send on its own.
5. **Accuracy.** Scripture, dates, names and facts are checked by a person before anything is published.
6. **Privacy.** People's information stays in the church's own systems. Prayer requests and personal
   details are never pasted into public AI tools.
7. **Honesty.** If someone asks whether AI helped write something, we tell them.
8. **Sermons.** The pastor decides how AI is used in sermon preparation, if at all.
9. **Who to ask.** The staff point person; this policy is reviewed once a year.
