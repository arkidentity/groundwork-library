# Kickoff call guide (team only, 60 minutes)

The call does the assessment. The written questions only get it started. Goal: free the pastor to
make disciples and pour into people. Leave with a drain list, every item in a bucket, and the first
one or two builds. Record it (with permission); file notes and the transcript in `notes/`.

| Bucket | Means | Ends up as |
|---|---|---|
| **Automate** | A system does it | A file in `automations/` |
| **Delegate** | Someone else does it, from a short manual | A manual and a named owner |
| **Own** | Only the pastor should, and it's worth their time | Protected time |
| **Drop** | We stop doing it | A line saying why |

1. **Ground rules (5 min).** Nothing goes out without approval. People's information stays in your
   tools. You own everything. No passwords in writing.
2. **What drains you (25 min).** Start from their q-drains answer. Walk last week, Monday to Sunday.
   What do you dread? What do people bring you that someone else could answer? What falls through
   the cracks? Write each task into the task audit as they talk.
3. **Sort it (15 min).** Each task into a bucket. Ask of each one: does this need *you*?
4. **Pick one or two (10 min).** The first builds, an owner on the church side for each, and a date.
5. **Tools as needed (5 min).** Only for the builds you picked.

If they feel overwhelmed, say so out loud and shrink it: one thing this month. Ask on the call,
never in writing: staff conflicts, money trouble, care cases, anything about a specific person.

After the call: fill the task audit, then share "Your plan" (one page) as a review.
