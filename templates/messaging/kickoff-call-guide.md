# Kickoff call guide (team only)

60 minutes, recorded and transcribed into `input/YYYY-MM-DD-kickoff-call.md`. Same ground as the
Workspace interview, live. The call catches what a screen can't: tone, hesitation, the story they
tell twice, the thing they won't write down.

## Before the call
- Read `input/` (public research) and anything they've sent.
- Pick the 3 gaps that matter most from the research's "Couldn't find" list.
- Anything sensitive (bad reviews, a partner dispute, money trouble) belongs on the call, never
  in a written question.

## Rules while you're on it
- **Collect, don't pitch.** No candidate lines, no "what if your tagline said…". Harvest only.
- **Ask for stories, not opinions.** "Tell me about a time…" beats "What do you think about…".
- **Chase their exact words.** When they say something vivid, ask them to say it again.
- **Let silence work.** The second answer is usually the real one.

## Flow

| Min | Topic | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | How'd you get into this? (Let them run.) |
| 5–15 | The customer | Your favorite customer. What was going on when they called? What almost stopped them? |
| 15–25 | The problem | What do people say in the first two minutes? What have they been through before you? What makes you angry about your industry? |
| 25–35 | What you do | Walk me through a job, call to last day. What do you do that others skip? |
| 35–45 | Proof and story | A job you're proud of. What do customers say at the end? What numbers can you stand behind? |
| 45–55 | The hard stuff | The 3 gaps from research. Anything sensitive, asked plainly. |
| 55–60 | Words | Explain what you do to a neighbor. Words you'd never use. |

## After the call
File the transcript in `input/` with a summary at the top. Pull 8–12 real sentences into the brand
brief's word list. Note which Workspace conversations the call already covered, so we don't ask twice.
